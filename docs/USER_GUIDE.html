Quick Start Guide
Go from sign-up to sent campaign in under an hour
Welcome. This one-pager walks you through the 24 things every pharmacy team should know on day one — from setting up your brand kit to sending your first campaign and putting posters on a screen in the shop.
Get started
1
Create your account Sign up with email + password. No credit card needed for the Free plan.
2
Complete onboarding Three quick steps — pharmacy details, contact, plan. Or Skip for now and finish later.
3
Set up your brand kit Logo, colours and footer — set once, applied everywhere.
4
Explore templates 24 designs: flu, vitamin D, hand hygiene, allergies and more.
Design posters
5
Use a template Click Use template to open the drag-and-drop editor with your brand pre-applied.
6
Customise on canvas Edit text, resize shapes, swap images. Auto-saves as you go; press Done when finished.
7
Apply pharmacy info One click inserts your name, address and phone into template placeholders.
8
Publish it Draft while you work; Publish when ready — only then can it be sent. PDF via the ⋯ menu.
Generate marketing videos
9
Type a topic, add your photos e.g. "Why book your flu jab this autumn". Attach up to four of your own photos and drag or zoom each to frame it.
10
Choose your voice Five free voices or premium ElevenLabs voices on Pro. Preview cost before you generate.
11
Wait about two minutes Research, script, voice and edit — a 15-second 9:16 clip.
12
Change a shot, free Ask for a change, tap the shot, say what it should show. Then download for Reels, TikTok, Shorts or a campaign.
Send campaigns
13
Add your patients Add them by hand or import a spreadsheet. Both optional.
14
Group your audience Group by condition or demographic for targeted sends.
15
Build a campaign Pick a published poster, attach up to 5 videos, choose patients and groups, name it.
16
One-click send The poster is in the email itself — video links live 48 hours.
Screens in the shop
17
Build a playlist Order published posters and set seconds on each.
18
Pair the screen once Open /display on the TV and type the six-character code.
19
Change it from your desk Edit the playlist and save. Screens follow within 30 seconds.
20
Turn off the TV's sleep timer The step people forget. Starter gets 1 screen, Pro unlimited.
Share, track & scale
21
Post to social, on repeat Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and TikTok — once or on repeat. Starter and Pro.
22
Analytics dashboard Open and click rates, top posters and videos. Pro plan.
23
Watch monthly usage Counters for posters, videos and recipients used this month.
24
Upgrade any time Free → Starter £7 → Pro £25 a month. No watermark.
