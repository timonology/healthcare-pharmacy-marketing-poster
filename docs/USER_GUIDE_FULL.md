## 1. Your account

### Signing up

You need an email address and a password. The Free plan does not ask for a
card, and it does not expire.

A welcome email follows. If it does not arrive, check your spam folder.

### Signing in and out

Signing out ends your session in that browser. If you were working as a branch
at the time (chapter 12), that ends too.

Every password box has a show/hide control. Use it to check what you typed
before submitting.

### If you forget your password

Press **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and enter your address. A link
arrives by email and opens a page where you set a new password.

The link expires after a short time and works once. Request another if you are
too late.

Two things happen when you reset:

- You are signed out on every device. Sign in again with the new password.
- A second email confirms the change. If you receive one you did not ask for,
  someone else has access to your mailbox. Contact support.

The page shows the same message whether or not the address has an account.

### Deactivated accounts

A deactivated account cannot sign in. Its data is kept. Contact support to
restore it.

## 2. Setting up your pharmacy

Onboarding has three steps: your pharmacy's details, contact information, and
a plan.

### Skipping it

**Skip for now** takes you into the app straight away. The dashboard then shows
what is still missing.

The skip lasts for that visit. Next time you sign in you are returned to setup,
and will be until you finish it. Press **Finish setup** on the dashboard prompt
to go there directly.

Until you complete it, posters and campaign emails go out without your pharmacy
name. Campaigns still send; the From line just will not identify you.

On posters, the spaces for your name, address and phone are simply left blank
until then — in the editor and in every PDF, image, email and screen. Nothing
is lost: as soon as you finish setup, every poster you have already made fills
in with your details automatically.

### Where your details appear

| Field | Used for |
|---|---|
| Pharmacy name | The From line on campaigns, poster placeholders, the header |
| Address | Poster placeholders, and optionally on a wall screen |
| Phone | Poster placeholders |
| Postcode | Your record |

## 3. The brand kit

**Brand Kit** holds your logo, colours, pharmacy details and regulatory footer.
Set it once and it is applied to every template you open.

### The logo

Upload a PNG, JPG or SVG. On a new account you can upload before saving the
rest of the kit.

SVG logos stay sharp at any size and are converted to a picture when a poster
is drawn.

### Colours

Set a palette. Templates are built so colours can be swapped without breaking
the design.

### Pharmacy details and footer

Name, address, contact details and licence number. The regulatory footer is
added to every poster automatically.

**Licence number is required.** If a save is refused, the message names the
field that is missing.

## 4. Templates

**Templates** holds 24 ready designs covering vaccination, promotion,
awareness, safety, seasonal and general subjects. Browse by category or by page
size.

**Use template** opens a copy in the editor with your brand kit applied. The
original is never changed.

### Design with AI

**Posters → Design with AI** designs a new poster from a sentence or two, in
the same modern style as the built-in templates, with your pharmacy's name,
phone number, address, logo and booking QR already on it. Say what it is for
and who it is for — *"walk-in flu clinic, bold, no appointment needed"*. You
can name a layout: circle portrait, diagonal, wave or checklist. Saying
"bold" makes the type larger in whichever layout is used.

Choose the photograph:

| Option | What happens |
|---|---|
| Stock (default) | A stock photo. Search and pick one, or leave it and one is found to suit the poster |
| Upload | Your own photo — JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 12MB. Say what is in it so it is placed well |
| None | A clean design with no photo |
| AI image | A photo made for the poster. Only shown when it is available |

Photos are cropped to fit, never stretched. A file that is not a photo is
refused as soon as you pick it.

Each design uses one of your monthly AI designs (Free 3, Starter 50, Pro
unlimited), whichever photo you choose. The poster opens in the editor, ready
to change.

### Page sizes

| Size | Use |
|---|---|
| A4 | Window and counter posters. The default |
| A3 | Larger window displays |
| A5 | Counter cards and handouts |
| DL | Leaflets and brochures |

Each is available in portrait or landscape. Everything is drawn at 150 dpi, so
exports are print-ready.

## 5. The poster editor

### Working on a design

- Drag to move. Use the handles to resize.
- Select text and type to edit it.
- Use bring-forward and send-backward when items overlap.
- Undo and redo are available for every change.

The editor saves as you work. There is no Save button. Press **Done** when you
have finished.

### Apply pharmacy info

This fills every placeholder in the design with your name, address and phone in
one step.

### Exporting

The **...** menu offers PDF, at the page size you chose.

On the Free plan exports carry a watermark. Starter and Pro do not.

## 6. Draft, published, archived

Every poster is in one of three states.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Still being worked on. Visible only to you |
| Published | Finished and approved for patients to see |
| Archived | Retired. Kept for your records |

**Only published posters can be sent in a campaign or shown on a screen.**
Drafts and archived posters are both refused, with a message saying so.

Archive a poster to take it out of circulation without deleting it.

### Finding things in a list

The templates gallery, My Posters, Videos, Campaigns and Contacts share one
toolbar: **Sort** (newest or oldest first) and **Date** (all time, last 7 days,
last 30 days, this month, or a custom range of days). The choice is kept in the
page address, so a bookmarked or shared link opens the same view. If nothing
matches, *No results in this date range* offers to clear the filter.

## 7. Your contact list

**Patients** holds the people you can send campaigns to: name, email and phone.
No clinical information is stored.

### Adding people

Add contacts one at a time, or import a spreadsheet.

### Importing

The import produces a report showing which rows were added, which were skipped
and which were rejected, each with its row number and the reason.

Duplicates are matched on email **or** phone, so the same person listed twice
under different addresses is caught.

If the file would take you over your plan's limit, the report lists the rows
that did not fit.

### Groups

Group contacts by condition, demographic or anything else useful for targeting,
then send a campaign to a group instead of the whole list.

Some groups are supplied by Marketing25 and appear alongside your own. Member
counts always show your contacts only.

### Exporting

**Export** downloads your whole list, not just the page on screen.

If a contact's name begins with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@`, the export puts a hidden
tab character in front of it so Excel treats it as text rather than a formula.
A leading space in a cell is normal.

## 8. Email campaigns

### Building one

Choose a published poster, attach up to five videos if you want, pick your
recipients — a group, individual contacts, or addresses typed in — and name the
campaign.

Recipients are checked before sending. A malformed address is rejected and
named.

One campaign can go to 1,000 people. Above 200 you are asked to confirm the
number before it is accepted.

### What recipients get

The poster appears in the body of the email, not as an attachment. The email
also contains:

- Your pharmacy name in the From line and footer
- Video thumbnails linking to playable versions, if you attached any
- A plain-text version for readers who block images

It is laid out to fit a phone screen without sideways scrolling.

Video links work for 48 hours.

The poster is loaded from a link rather than being packed into the email
itself. That is what makes a campaign to hundreds of people possible: the same
design packed into every copy is what used to make a send to four people time
out. Most email apps show it straight away. A few — Outlook on a work computer
is the common one — hold images back until the reader allows them, so a
**Not showing?** link sits under the poster and the plain-text version carries
the address. The link keeps working for six months.

### While it sends

Pressing Send accepts the campaign rather than finishing it. The status goes to
**Queued**, then **Sending**, then **Sent**, and the count of recipients reached
climbs as it goes. A thousand-recipient campaign takes a few minutes.

You can close the tab. The send continues on the server and the campaign will
be in its final state when you come back. If the server restarts partway
through, the campaign resumes and nobody is emailed twice.

**Stop** halts a campaign that is queued or sending. Email already handed to the
mail provider cannot be recalled, so the count may rise by up to 20 after you
press it, and then stops. The delivery report shows exactly who was reached.
Nobody else is contacted, and a stopped campaign stays stopped.

### After sending

The delivery report lists every recipient with accepted or failed status,
timestamps, and any message from the mail provider.

**Retry** sends to the people the campaign did not reach, and only to them.
It is offered on a campaign that failed outright and on one that reached some
of its list but not all of it. A campaign everybody received cannot be retried.

### Limits

Recipients per month: Free none, Starter 500, Pro 5,000. Free accounts cannot
send campaigns and are shown an upgrade prompt instead.

The allowance is counted when you press Send, not when the campaign finishes,
so queueing several large campaigns at once cannot take you past it.

## 9. Marketing videos

**Videos** turns a topic into a 15-second vertical clip for Reels, TikTok or
YouTube Shorts.

### Making one

Enter a topic, such as *why book your flu jab this autumn*. Choose how the
script is written:

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| AI script | Researches and writes it for you. Fastest |
| Guided | Writes to creative direction you provide |
| My script | Uses narration you supply word for word |

Choose a voice from the list offered. The list reflects what your pharmacy's
system is actually set up to use, so every voice shown is one you will get —
there is no longer a choice that quietly gets swapped for another. The estimated
cost per video is shown before you generate.

Generation takes 30 to 60 seconds. Progress is shown as it researches, writes,
narrates and edits.

Scripts avoid scaremongering and dosing advice, and frame anything about
prescriptions as *ask your pharmacist*.

### Using your own photos

You can attach up to four of your own pictures — your shopfront, your team,
your logo, or a poster you have already published. They appear in the video
itself: the first one opens it, the next closes it over the *come and see us*
line, and any others are spread through the middle. Every other shot is stock
footage, as before.

Your photos are used exactly as they are. Nothing is generated from them and
nothing is sent to an image generator, so **there is no extra charge** — this is
not the same thing as the AI imagery option, which is a separate switch.

JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 8MB each. Photos taken on a phone are fine, whichever
way up you held it. Remove one by pressing the × on its thumbnail before you
generate.

**Framing each photo.** The video is tall and narrow, so a wide photo does not
fit as it is. Each thumbnail shows exactly what the video will show:

- **Drag** the photo to choose which part is kept. It only moves in a
  direction where there is more picture to see.
- **Zoom** (the slider, a two-finger pinch, or Ctrl + scroll) makes it smaller
  so more of it fits — down to half size. The space around it is filled with a
  softened copy of the same photo, never black bars, and nothing is stretched.
  Zooming above 100% crops in closer, up to 150%.
- **Reset** puts it back to the centre at 100%.

Leave it alone and you get what every photo has always had: the centre,
filling the frame. The same controls appear when you add photos to a finished
video.

If one of your pictures cannot be read, that moment simply uses stock footage
instead and the rest of the video is made as normal — you will not lose the
video or the allowance it used.

**You can also add photos after the video is made.** Open **Ask for a change**
on a finished video and choose *Or add your own pictures*. They are added to any
the video already has, up to four in total, and the video is re-cut with them
in. The words, the voice and the rest of the footage are kept, it does not use
another video from your monthly allowance, and it costs nothing.

The one exception is a video made with AI-generated imagery. Adding your photo
to one of those would mean generating all of its other pictures again, which
your plan pays for, so it is not offered — make a new video with your
photographs attached instead.

### Changing a shot in a finished video

Open **Ask for a change**. Every shot of the video is shown as a tile, with
its time and the words spoken over it. Tap the shot you want to change (up to
six at once), type what it should show — "a person stepping on a scale" — and
press **Change this shot and re-cut**. Leave the box empty to get something on
the video's own topic. Your own photos are marked; tapping one replaces it with
footage at that point.

It costs nothing and does not use a video from your allowance: the words and
the voice stay exactly as they are, only the footage at those shots changes.
If nothing in the stock library matches what you asked for, another shot from
the same video is used there rather than something unrelated.

You can also type the change, naming the shot by position: "change the last
shot to people walking", "shot 2 to a salad". We don't pick a shot from a
description of what is in it — tap it instead.

### If a render fails

Press retry. Retries do not count against your monthly allowance.

### Limits

Videos per month: Free none, Starter 5, Pro 30. AI-generated imagery is
available on Pro only.

## 10. Social media and Google

**Social & Ads** connects your accounts, publishes posters and videos, and runs
recurring posts. It is on Starter and Pro, not Free.

| Account | Posters | Videos | How you connect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook Page | Yes | Yes | **Facebook & Instagram** |
| Instagram Business or Creator | Yes | Yes | **Facebook & Instagram** |
| YouTube channel | No | Yes, as Shorts | **YouTube** |
| LinkedIn profile | Yes | Yes | **LinkedIn** |
| LinkedIn company Page | Yes | Yes | **LinkedIn**, once enabled on your server |
| TikTok account | No | Yes | **TikTok** |
| X account | Yes | Yes | **X** |
| Google Ads | Advertising only, not a posting target | | **Google Ads** |

Google Business Profile posting is not available yet.

- **Instagram** is connected through the Facebook Page it is linked to. If it
  does not appear, check that link first. Posters go to Instagram as a 4:5
  version made for it.
- **YouTube** takes videos only, so posters are not offered for it. The first
  line of your caption becomes the video title (up to 100 characters).
- **LinkedIn** asks you to sign in again every **60 days**. Two weeks before,
  the account shows *The sign-in expires on …* with a **Reconnect** button.
  Company Pages appear when you sign in, if you are an admin or content admin
  of the Page.

- **TikTok** takes videos only. Each post asks who can see it (from the
  choices TikTok offers your account — nothing is pre-selected), whether others
  may comment, Duet or Stitch (all off to start), and whether it is commercial
  content ("Your brand" or "Branded content", which cannot be private). One
  TikTok account per post, and TikTok is not available for recurring posts.
  Until TikTok approves Marketing25, TikTok posts are private and the TikTok
  account itself must be set to private.
- **X** allows **280** per post, counted X's way: each link counts as 23
  and each emoji as 2. When X is ticked, an *X: n / 280* line under the
  caption shows the count, and Publish waits until it fits. X refuses a post
  identical to one already on the account, so vary the wording.

### Connecting and reconnecting

Press **Connect** on the account type and sign in at the platform. You then
choose which accounts to connect — everything found is ticked; untick any you
do not want. Accounts already connected are renewed in place, so scheduled and
recurring posts to them carry on.

An account that needs reconnecting is marked **Reconnect**: after a password
change, a removed admin role, or an expired sign-in. Each account is checked
once a day, so this usually shows before anything is due. Posts to it fail with
that reason until you reconnect; other accounts still get them.

**Disconnect** (the bin icon) deletes our copy of the account's sign-in at once.
Scheduled posts to it will fail, and recurring posts stop going to it.

### Publishing once

On a published poster or a finished video, choose **Share to social**, tick the
accounts, write a caption and choose **Publish now** or a time. Accounts that
cannot take that kind of post are left out.

Posts appear under **Posts** (Upcoming and History) with a link to each post
once it is live. A scheduled post can be rescheduled or cancelled; one a
platform refused can be retried.

**Uncertain** means the platform did not confirm — the connection dropped while
it was posting. Check the account before pressing Retry, or it may appear twice.

### Recurring posts

On **Starter (up to 2 active)** and **Pro (up to 20)**. Set up from the
**Recurring** tab, or **Make recurring** on a poster or video, in four steps:

1. **What** — a poster or video.
2. **Where** — Facebook, Instagram, YouTube (videos) or LinkedIn. Not X
   (it refuses the same text twice) and not TikTok,
   which asks for each post to be chosen by a person.
3. **When** — daily, chosen weekdays, every few days, or specific dates, at a
   time in your time zone. Clock changes are handled: 09:00 stays 09:00.
4. **Review** — the next dates exactly as they will happen.

The list shows each series, its next post, recent results and a 14-day
calendar. **Pause** stops future posts (history is kept), **Resume** carries on,
**End** stops it for good. **Edit** changes future posts only.

- A post that could not go out within two hours of its time is skipped, not
  sent late, and a week of downtime never sends a burst of posts.
- If one account needs reconnecting, only that account misses the post.
- A series pauses itself if none of its accounts are usable, the poster is no
  longer published, or the plan no longer includes it.

Emails tell you when a post goes out, when one fails or an account needs
reconnecting, and when a series pauses itself — or only about problems, if you
choose.

### Google Ads

Connect the Google account that owns the Ads account, then create a local
campaign from a poster. Campaigns are created **paused**; you start them.
Read *Healthcare advertising* in the setup guide before enabling one —
prescription-only medicines cannot be advertised to the public.

## 11. Screens in the pharmacy

A television or tablet on the wall showing a loop of your published posters and
finished videos.

Available on Starter (one screen) and Pro (unlimited). Not on Free.

### The three pieces

| | What it is | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Screen | One physical panel | **Screens** |
| Playlist | An ordered set of posters (a dwell time each) and videos (played once each) | **Playlists** |
| Kiosk | The page the device opens | `/display` |

`/display` is the television. `/displays` is where you manage them.

A playlist can be shown on more than one screen, so one edit reaches all of
them.

### Setting one up

**1. Publish your posters.** Screens show published posters only.

**2. Build a playlist.** Add posters, set the seconds for each, and reorder
with the arrows.

- Between 3 and 300 seconds each. The default is 12.
- Up to 50 items.
- The same item cannot appear twice in a row.
- Nothing reaches a screen until you press **Save**.

**Adding a video.** Press **Add video** and choose from your finished videos.
Only videos that are *Ready* are listed, and a playlist can hold up to 10.

- A video plays **once**, for its own length, then the loop moves on.
- It is always **muted** — screens never play sound.
- A portrait video on a landscape screen shows as a centred column with black
  either side. It is never stretched or cropped.
- If you change a video, or it is deleted, it leaves your screens within about
  thirty seconds and shows *Not ready — skipped* in the playlist. A changed
  video comes back on its own when it is ready.
- The Screens page shows a small badge with the number of videos a screen is
  set to play.

**3. Register the screen.** Go to **Screens → Add screen**.

- **Name** — how you tell it from others, such as *Waiting room*.
- **Where it hangs** — for your records. Not displayed.
- **Address shown on the screen** — optional, drawn along the bottom.

Then choose the playlist from the **Showing** dropdown.

**4. Pair the device.** On the television, open
`https://<your-domain>/display`. Read the six-character code from the Screens
page and type it in.

Codes last four hours and work once. They leave out characters that are easily
misread: `O`/`0`, `I`/`1`/`L`, `S`/`5`, `Z`/`2`, `B`/`8` and `U`/`V`. Press
**New code** if one expires.

The loop starts within about thirty seconds.

**5. Set up the device.**

- Turn off the device's sleep timer in its own settings. The page asks the
  browser to keep the screen awake, but some devices refuse.
- Set the browser to open `/display` on boot, so a power cut does not need a
  visit.
- Run it full screen.
- Use landscape unless your posters are portrait.

### Changing what a screen shows

Edit the playlist and press **Save**. Screens pick up the change on their next
poll, within about thirty seconds. Nothing needs restarting.

Assigning a different playlist works the same way.

Unpublishing a poster removes it from every screen showing it, on the next
poll. The playlist keeps the entry, struck through and labelled *Not published
— skipped*.

### Access, and taking it away

There are two kinds of credential.

**Paired device** is the normal one, created by typing a code at the device.
It is stored in that browser.

**Direct link** is for hardware that cannot take a code, such as a screen with
no usable keyboard. Press **Direct link** and copy the URL. It is shown once.

**Revoke** stops either one. The device returns to the pairing screen on its
next poll.

- Pairing a new device replaces the previous paired device.
- A direct link does not affect a paired device, or the reverse.
- Issuing a new direct link retires the previous one.

### On a bad connection

The screen keeps the last playlist it downloaded, poster images included, and
carries on looping. Videos are not stored on the device, so one the screen has
not played recently is skipped until the connection returns. A small amber dot appears in the corner.

A screen that has been unplugged and moved starts showing its cached loop
before it reaches the network.

### Checking on a screen

The **Last seen** column shows the state of each screen. Screens poll every
thirty seconds.

| Shows | Means |
|---|---|
| Live | Heard from in the last two minutes |
| Last seen 3 h ago | Switched off, asleep, or off the network |
| Paired, not yet connected | The code was used but the device has never connected. Usually a network problem at the device |

## 12. Running several branches

One login can work across several pharmacies, for an area manager, a locum or a
head office.

### Switching

The pharmacy you are working as is named in the header at all times, and shown
in amber when it is not your own.

Anything you create while switched — posters, campaigns, screens — belongs to
that pharmacy. Switch back the same way.

### Branches

A head office sees its branches under **Branches**. Adding one creates the
account and sends an activation email to the address you provide.

Opening a branch shows its records, and the fact that you opened it is recorded
in the audit log.

You cannot open a branch that is not yours.

### Asking for access

You can request access to a pharmacy by its email address, giving a reason. A
Marketing25 administrator approves or rejects the request and can send a note
back.

The request grants nothing on its own. Approval lets you work as that pharmacy.
It does not let you pass that access to anyone else.

### Screens across branches

To manage a branch's screens, switch to that branch first, then add them.
Screens belong to whichever pharmacy was active when they were created.

## 14. Privacy and who can see what

### Your data

A pharmacy sees only its own records. Posters, campaigns, contacts, playlists
and screens are all restricted to the account, and a link to another pharmacy's
record is refused.

The one exception is a head office working as a branch (chapter 12), which is
named in the header and recorded in the audit log.

### What Marketing25 staff can see

Support staff can work as your pharmacy to help you. Every session is recorded
with who did it, which pharmacy, and when it started and ended.

**Support cannot see patient contact details.** They can see that a campaign
went to 240 people. They cannot read the 240 addresses.

### What a wall screen receives

A screen receives the screen's name, the address line if you configured one,
and images of the posters on its own playlist.

It does not receive your email address, your account details, patient data, or
anything about any other screen.

### Social accounts you connect

We hold each connected account's sign-in (encrypted), its name, picture and id,
and the links to posts we published. We do not read followers, messages or
insights. Disconnecting deletes the sign-in at once.

### Contact data

Contacts are marketing data with no clinical fields. The pharmacy, not
Marketing25, is the data controller. The full terms and privacy policy are
linked from the sign-up page and the site footer.

## 15. When something goes wrong

### Posters and templates

| Symptom | Cause |
|---|---|
| A download opens a blank tab or shows `null` | Something failed on the server. Report it |
| Your logo is missing from a poster | Check the brand kit still has it, then reopen the poster |
| The export has a watermark | You are on the Free plan |
| A poster cannot be added to a campaign or screen | It is a draft or archived. Publish it first |

### Campaigns

| Symptom | Cause |
|---|---|
| The email has no pharmacy name | Onboarding or the brand kit is unfinished |
| The poster is missing from the email | The design failed to draw. Report it with the campaign name |
| A campaign to many recipients failed | Report it with the recipient count |
| Recipients rejected before sending | A malformed address. The message names it |
| Cannot send at all | Free accounts have no campaign allowance |

### Screens

| Symptom | Cause |
|---|---|
| *That code is not valid any more* | Codes last four hours and work once. Press **New code** |
| *No posters assigned* | No playlist on the screen, or the playlist is empty |
| The screen is blank | The playlist holds only unpublished posters. Look for struck-through entries |
| The loop got shorter | A poster was unpublished or deleted |
| An amber dot in the corner | The screen is running on its cached playlist with no connection |
| Back at the pairing box | The credential was revoked, or the screen was deleted |
| *In-pharmacy screens are not part of the Free plan* | Upgrade to Starter or Pro |
| The screen sleeps overnight | The device's own sleep timer. Turn it off in the device settings |

### Social & Ads

| Symptom | Cause |
|---|---|
| An account shows **Reconnect** | The platform no longer accepts the sign-in — a password change, a removed Page role, or (LinkedIn) the 60-day sign-in ran out. Press **Reconnect** |
