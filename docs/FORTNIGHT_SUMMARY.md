# Two weeks of delivery — summary

**13 to 26 September.** Full detail in `FORTNIGHT_REVIEW.md`.

- **Change a shot of a finished video by tapping it.** *Ask for a change* shows every shot as a tile: a still of the shot as rendered, its time, and the words spoken over it. Tap up to six, say what each should show, and the video is re-cut. It is free, keeps the voice and the words, and does not use a video from the allowance. A typed request may name a shot only by position ("the last shot", "shot 3"). The system never guesses a shot from a description. That guessing is what refused a real request with "no moment shows medicine".
- **Why it took a structural fix.** Change requests failed one after another this week: every typed change crashed; refusals were invisible; shots were matched to the wrong moments; footage repeated; a stock shot was refused as "your own photo". Underneath all of them, **the shot list the refine used was not the shots in the video**. When narration ran long, the editor appended filler clips that belonged to no moment. Now every shot on screen is a moment, and the pharmacist picks it.
- **The pharmacy's own photographs in videos**: up to four, at create or afterwards, free. Each can be dragged and zoomed, with a blurred fill rather than black bars, and the thumbnail is exactly what the video shows. Fixed on the way: framing chosen at create was being dropped, and upright phone photos rendered sideways.
- **Hosted video could never narrate.** The provider was chosen from the voice id, the default voice was the free one, and that provider is refused from datacentre networks. A configured paid key now means the paid voice, with no silent fallback.
- **Length: 27s, then 22.8s, for a 15-second product.** The word budget is now derived from the ordered length *and* enforced after writing. An over-long script is tightened once, keeping the hook and the call to action.
- **Posters designed by AI**, with the plan limits (Free 3, Starter 50, Pro unlimited) enforced for the first time. They had been advertised from the start and checked nowhere.
- **Security:** a pharmacy could name another pharmacy's file as a video attachment, download it inside a video, and destroy it on delete. Every key must now belong to the caller.
- **The page freeze** was two copies of a Radix module fighting over the page's pointer lock. Pinned, with a build check against a second copy.
- **`pnpm smoke`** makes one real call through every paid door of a running deployment. It now also changes a video and proves: the tapped shot changed and nothing else; the voice was reused; the allowance did not move. Its self-test has 19 fake deployments, each broken in one way.
- **Testing: 631 .NET tests passing, 234 ai-service test functions, 42 web tests (the first ever), 10 build checks, 147 manual QA cases.** New rule: a regression test only counts once it has been seen to fail with its bug back. **`pnpm test:prove` reintroduces 50 fixed bugs and catches all 50.** `REGRESSION-TESTS.md` maps every fixed bug to its test.
- **Decisions needed:** **commit and push first.** Production runs build `791cb3f-dirty`, the last commit is 11 September, and 210 files exist only on a laptop and in the running images. Then: rotate the mail credentials still in the repository (carried since August); decide whether rewriting a video's words is allowed (recommended: yes, as one video from the allowance); and set a ceiling on AI spend across the whole estate.

# Two weeks of delivery — summary

**30 August to 12 September.** Full detail in `FORTNIGHT_REVIEW.md`.

- **Campaigns now work at the size we sell them.** Our mail provider carries a separate copy of the poster inside every recipient's message, so a thousand-recipient campaign was a 244 MB upload that could not complete — the failing log read 977,045 bytes for four messages. The poster now travels as a signed link instead: **3.7 KB per recipient, 3.7 MB for a thousand.** Sending is queued rather than held in the request, campaigns above 200 ask for confirmation, and a single campaign is capped at 1,000.
- **The link points at the image, not the poster**, so editing a poster next month doesn't change what an email sent today shows. Links last 180 days.
- **Fixed: Stop did not stop.** A pharmacist pressing Stop saw it accepted; the campaign ran to the last recipient and reported itself Sent. The worker's progress writes were replacing the whole record and erasing the Stop. It now halts within one group — stated precisely, because a group already handed to the mail provider will finish.
- **Fixed: the monthly allowance could be walked straight past.** Only finished campaigns counted, so five 500-recipient campaigns created in the same minute on a 500-a-month plan would all go.
- **Fixed: Retry was missing on exactly the campaigns that needed it.** It appeared only on campaigns marked Failed, and a campaign that reached 700 of 1,000 is marked Sent — so the 300 who got nothing had no route except a new campaign that would reach the first 700 twice.
- **Fixed: deploying during a send marked campaigns Failed**, in a state nothing could pick up again. An ordinary Tuesday release would have stranded whatever was in flight.
- **Posters on a screen in the pharmacy.** A television or tablet loops published posters — a playlist, one web address, a six-character code typed once. No app and no account on the device. Edits reach every screen within thirty seconds, and a screen keeps looping through a wifi outage. The device's credential is a key to one playlist and nothing else. Free 0 / Starter 1 / Pro unlimited.
- **A crashed video render is recoverable.** Renders now hold a lease with an expiry. If the render service restarts or runs out of memory, the video becomes retryable **without charging the pharmacy's monthly allowance a second time**. Previously it stayed "Rendering" forever, already charged, with no way back for the customer.
- **Fixed: renaming a video destroyed its script.** The app was writing the whole video record from a copy that never held the render service's half — script, generated title, tags, cost record — so a rename silently erased all of it while appearing to work perfectly. Each side now writes only its own fields.
- **New: booking link and QR code.** A pharmacy stores a booking web address once; posters and shop-window screens can print it or draw a scannable code, so somebody on the pavement can book from the glass. Two faults found while building it: some spellings of the token printed as raw `{{...}}` on patient-facing posters, and completing onboarding silently wiped a booking link set earlier.
- **Fixed: a new administrator could not get into the admin console** — held at a pharmacy setup form with no pharmacy to set up. This would have hit the first production deployment, on the first administrator, before anything else could be done.
- **Testing: 449 API tests, 123 browser tests, 8 checks on every build, 107 manual QA cases** with a PDF, alongside a full user guide. A written account of *why* the suite stayed green through the email faults is in `TESTING.md` — the short version is that no test had ever asked what the recipient actually receives.
- **Every fault this fortnight was at a seam**, never inside a function: two lists that had to agree by hand, two write paths that had to agree about what silence means, two processes writing one record. That is now the first place we look.
- **Four decisions worth your input:** rotating the mail credentials still committed to the repository (most urgent, carried from three reports); whether Stop should be stronger than "within one group"; what refining a video should cost, which needs spend measured first; and whether a pharmacy that downgrades should lose its shop-window screens — today it keeps them.
